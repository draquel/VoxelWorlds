VoxelWorlds

Engine architecture · Unreal Engine 5.8 · C++ + compute shaders · state 2026-11-05

Eleven modules that turn noise into worlds.

A voxel terrain engine: generated on the GPU, meshed on CPU workers, streamed around the viewer with LOD, collision and compression, drawn by one renderer on UE's local vertex factory, and carried past the voxel window by an analytic far field, out to the horizon and up to orbit. Each module does one job and works with the others through interfaces, not direct coupling.

11 modules 3 world modes · plane, island, planet 3 meshers · cubic, MC, DC watertight seams by ownership far field to the horizon planets to ~1,000 km

Fig. B — Chunk lifecycle

From viewer to visible terrain

The path one chunk takes, from entering the voxel window to leaving it. The chunk manager (VoxelStreaming) drives every stage. Only generation runs on the GPU, with the CPU generator as its twin. Meshing runs on CPU workers in two independent kinds of job: a chunk's interior, and the seams it shares with its neighbours.

01 Window Each tick the LOD strategy turns the streaming viewer into the voxel window: which chunks to hold, and at which LOD. VoxelLOD
GPU · CPU twin 02 Generate One compute pass (density, biome, caves, ore), then the water and underground post-passes, read back. A uniform chunk collapses to one value. VoxelGeneration
CPU workers 03 Mesh the interior A worker meshes the chunk from its own voxels alone: cubic, Marching Cubes or Dual Contouring. VoxelMeshing
Seam ownership 04 Mesh the seams Each shared boundary is one seam job. Once every side has voxels, it reads their snapshots and meshes the boundary at both sides' LODs. Neither chunk waits on the other. FVoxelSeamRegistry
05 Draw Each mesh becomes a primitive in its render region, with no component per chunk. A dithered crossfade swaps it in for the mesh it replaces. VoxelRendering
06 Collide A Chaos trimesh is cooked near the player on the thread pool, reading neighbours as boundary shells. A neighbour that arrives later triggers a re-cook. UVoxelCollisionManager
07 Decorate Inside the scatter horizon, surface points are extracted from the voxels, placed per biome and drawn as HISM. Water tiles are drawn as a second pass. VoxelScatter · Rendering
08 Compress · unload A settled far chunk is encoded on a worker and its array dropped; readers get decoded snapshots. Past the unload horizon the chunk leaves every system. Core codec · Streaming
Edits A sparse overlay in UVoxelEditManager (add, subtract, set, paint; undo and redo for player edits, with system edits kept out of the history). For each chunk an edit changes, the chunk is re-meshed, its seams restaled, the collision cooks that read it re-cooked and its scatter re-extracted, and water floods whatever the edit opened under the sea.
Fig. C — World modes and the far field

Beyond the voxel window

Every world mode shares one pipeline. Past the voxel window, FVoxelFarField draws the conditioned analytic surface to the horizon. Its patches are split by screen-space error and built on the thread pool.

A band patch hides once every chunk it covers is drawn, so the window and the far field neither gap nor overlap. Above the cut-over altitude every chunk unloads; coming down, the viewer policy's descent lead streams the window back in ahead of the camera.

Infinite planeFInfinitePlaneWorldMode

A heightmap without bounds. The window streams each column's surface slab plus the viewer's layers; the far field is root-tile quadtrees on the XY grid.

Island / bowlEWorldMode::IslandBowl

A bounded heightmap with a seabed, and a far ocean of open sea past its edge.

Spherical planetFSphericalPlanetWorldMode

Radial terrain around a centre, up to ~1,000 km in radius, streamed by chunk along local up. The far field is six cube-face quadtrees; a small planet without a far-field sea draws a sea sphere.

Block worldsEMeshingMode::Cubic

Any heightmap mode can be meshed with cubic blocks. LOD is off; greedy meshing handles distance instead.

Precision by regions16³ chunks a region

Every primitive sits at its render region's double origin and stores float offsets from it, in every mode, so there is no world-origin shift.

Fig. D — Capability map

What the engine ships

What the engine ships, grouped by system. Colours use the same layer coding as the module map. Each system's details are in its Features doc.

Generation

  • GPU compute; the CPU generator is its twin, held to it by the GPUvsCPU*Parity tests
  • Hashed, split-origin FVoxelNoise: FBM and domain warp
  • Biomes by temperature, moisture and continentalness
  • Caves (cheese, spaghetti, noodle) and ore veins

World modes

  • Infinite plane, island / bowl, spherical planet
  • Swappable via IVoxelWorldMode
  • Cubic block worlds on any heightmap mode
  • Surface queries along local up, the same call on a planet

Meshing & seams

  • Cubic: greedy, face-culled
  • Marching Cubes: Transvoxel ribbon and geomorph at LOD seams
  • Dual Contouring: QEF solve, fitted normals
  • One seam job per boundary, watertight across LOD

LOD & far field

  • Distance bands, refine and coarsen hysteresis, 2:1 balance
  • Analytic far field in quadtree patches, to the horizon
  • The cut-over to orbit and back, with a descent lead

Streaming & memory

  • The 4-byte voxel: 64³ chunk = 1 MB resident
  • Immutable copy-on-write snapshots for workers
  • Uniform collapse and far compression: a ~1,100-chunk window settles near 130 MB
  • Async Chaos collision cooking near the player

Rendering

  • One renderer on FLocalVertexFactory
  • A primitive per chunk mesh, with no component per chunk
  • Dithered crossfades on every swap
  • Cached ray tracing, far shadow cascades, RVT output

Materials

  • Registry of up to 256 types; hardness per material
  • Packed atlas (cubic), Texture2DArrays with triplanar (smooth)
  • One M_VoxelMaster: slope-aware rock, texture variation
  • Ships on DX11 / SM5

Water

  • Water flags and a fill pass at generation
  • Cross-chunk pull; floods driven by edits
  • Water tiles per column, drawn as a second pass
  • The island's far ocean, the planet's sea

Scatter & foliage

  • Surface points from the voxels, never the mesh
  • Per-biome placement on the thread pool
  • Pooled HISM per scatter region, batched writes
  • Voxel trees; edit-aware re-extraction

Editing

  • Sparse overlay: add, subtract, set, paint
  • Undo and redo for player edits
  • System edits (POIs, dungeon stamps, floods) outside the history
  • Validated by the game through IVoxelEditValidator

Map & navigation

  • Async map tiles from the analytic height
  • Tile images under a fixed memory budget
  • The globe on a planet, edit overlays
  • Surface A* without a navmesh

PCG & editor tools

  • Runtime PCG over the surface, one graph per biome
  • Edit-merged sampling near chunks, the generator beyond
  • Field preview panel and actor, 8 built-in fields
  • The editor module is stripped from shipping
Fig. E — Design decisions

Why it is shaped this way

Each choice was measured or forced by the engine, not assumed. The full list is in ARCHITECTURE.md.

01

GPU generation, CPU meshing

Generation is one thread per voxel and costs the GPU a fraction of a millisecond per chunk. A chunk's mesh and the seams it meets have to agree exactly, which the GPU meshers never managed, so meshing runs on CPU workers.

the GPU meshers were deleted · the CPU generator stays as the GPU's twin and runs headless
02

Seams have one owner

One seam job owns each chunk boundary and reads both sides, rather than each chunk reading its neighbours' faces. Seams are watertight across LOD by construction, no chunk waits on a neighbour, and an edit re-meshes only the chunks it changed.

replaced the June LOD-boundary welds and the Transvoxel plan
03

One renderer, no Nanite

Nanite is built at cook time, and terrain generated at runtime from a seed cannot be Nanite. Every chunk mesh is a primitive on FLocalVertexFactory, so the engine culls it per view and shadow cascade and caches its ray tracing.

the custom vertex factory research and the PMC fallback were dropped
04

Precision by regions

Primitives sit at double-precision region origins (16³ chunks) and store float offsets. Generation keeps its large values in double or in the chunk's frame, and the noise is split-origin. Heightmaps are exact to a double's range, planets to about 1,000 km.

no world-origin rebasing, in any mode
05

Strategies behind interfaces

IVoxelLODStrategy, IVoxelWorldMode, IVoxelMesher and IVoxelMeshRenderer keep each piece swappable. One distance-band strategy has met every world mode, the planet included.

cubic worlds turn LOD off rather than add a strategy
06

Mode-generic by default

A feature built for one world mode moves to all of them once it applies: the far field, the cut-over, regions, the noise. Its name carries no mode. World Partition streams placed actors only; the terrain streams itself.

FVoxelFarField · FVoxelNoise · VoxelCutoverAltitude
