VoxelWorlds

Engine architecture · Unreal Engine 5.8 · C++ + compute shaders

Eleven modules that turn noise into worlds.

A voxel terrain engine: generated on the GPU, meshed on CPU workers, streamed around the viewer with LOD, collision and compression, drawn by one renderer on UE's local vertex factory, and carried past the voxel window by an analytic far field, out to the horizon and up to orbit.

11 modules 3 world modes · plane, island, planet 3 meshers · cubic, MC, DC watertight seams by ownership far field to the horizon planets to ~1,000 km

Chunk lifecycle

From viewer to visible terrain

03 / 07 · the chunk manager drives every stage
01 Window Each tick the LOD strategy turns the streaming viewer into the voxel window: which chunks to hold, and at which LOD. VoxelLOD
GPU · CPU twin 02 Generate One compute pass (density, biome, caves, ore), then water and underground post-passes, read back. A uniform chunk collapses to one value. VoxelGeneration
CPU workers 03 Mesh the interior A worker meshes the chunk from its own voxels alone: cubic, Marching Cubes or Dual Contouring. VoxelMeshing
seam ownership 04 Mesh the seams Each shared boundary is one seam job. It reads both sides' snapshots and meshes at both LODs, so the seam is watertight and neither chunk waits. Streaming · Meshing
05 Draw Each mesh becomes a primitive in its render region. A dithered crossfade swaps it in for the mesh it replaces. VoxelRendering
06 Collide A Chaos trimesh is cooked near the player on the thread pool, reading neighbours as boundary shells. VoxelStreaming
07 Decorate Inside the scatter horizon, surface points come from the voxels, get placed per biome and drawn as HISM. Water tiles are drawn in a second pass. VoxelScatter · Rendering
08 Compress · unload A settled far chunk is encoded and its array dropped; readers get decoded snapshots. Past the unload horizon it leaves every system. Core codec · Streaming
Edits A sparse overlay in UVoxelEditManager (add, subtract, set, paint; undo and redo). For each chunk an edit changes, the chunk is re-meshed, its seams restaled, its collision readers re-cooked and its scatter re-extracted, and water floods whatever the edit opened.

World modes · the far field

Beyond the voxel window

04 / 07 · one pipeline, three worlds, to the horizon

A band patch hides once every chunk it covers is drawn, so the window and the far field neither gap nor overlap. Above the cut-over every chunk unloads; coming down, the window streams back in ahead of the descent.

Infinite planeFInfinitePlaneWorldMode

A heightmap without bounds. The far field is root-tile quadtrees on the XY grid.

Island / bowlIslandBowl

A bounded heightmap with a seabed, and a far ocean of open sea past its edge.

Spherical planetFSphericalPlanetWorldMode

Radial terrain around a centre, up to ~1,000 km in radius. The far field is six cube-face quadtrees, and you can fly to orbit and back.

Block worldsEMeshingMode::Cubic

Any heightmap mode can be meshed with cubic blocks. LOD is off; greedy meshing handles distance instead.

Precision by regions16³ chunks a region

Every primitive sits at its render region's double origin and stores float offsets from it. There is no world-origin shift in any mode.

Design decisions

Why it is shaped this way

06 / 07 · each one measured or forced, not assumed
01 GPU generation, CPU meshing

Generation is one thread per voxel and costs the GPU a fraction of a millisecond per chunk. A chunk's mesh and its seams have to agree exactly, which the GPU meshers never managed, so meshing runs on CPU workers.

the GPU meshers were deleted · the CPU generator stays as the GPU's twin
02 Seams have one owner

One job owns each chunk boundary and reads both sides. Seams are watertight across LOD by construction, no chunk waits on a neighbour, and an edit re-meshes only what it changed.

replaced the LOD-boundary welds and the Transvoxel plan
03 One renderer, no Nanite

Nanite is built at cook time, and this terrain is generated at runtime from a seed. Every chunk mesh is a primitive on FLocalVertexFactory, so the engine culls it per view and caches its ray tracing.

the custom vertex factory and the PMC fallback were dropped
04 Precision by regions

Primitives sit at double-precision region origins and store float offsets. Generation keeps its large values in double or in the chunk's frame. Heightmaps are exact to a double's range, planets to about 1,000 km.

no world-origin rebasing, in any mode
05 Strategies behind interfaces

IVoxelLODStrategy, IVoxelWorldMode, IVoxelMesher and IVoxelMeshRenderer keep each piece swappable. One distance-band strategy has met every mode, the planet included.

cubic worlds turn LOD off rather than add a strategy
06 Mode-generic by default

A feature built for one world mode moves to all of them once it applies: the far field, the cut-over, regions, the noise. Its name carries no mode.

FVoxelFarField · FVoxelNoise · VoxelCutoverAltitude
